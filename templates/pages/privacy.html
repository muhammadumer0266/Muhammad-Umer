Privacy

What this site collects, and why.

The contact form

Submitting the contact form stores your name, email address and message so a reply can be sent. Your IP address and browser user agent are never stored in raw form — only a salted, one-way hash of each, used solely to apply spam and rate-limit protections. Messages are kept to answer them and are not shared with anyone else or used for any other purpose.

Résumé downloads

Downloading the résumé asks for your email address. It is stored so {{ SITE_NAME }} can see who requested the résumé and, if relevant, follow up once. It is never sold, shared, or added to a mailing list. As with the contact form, your IP address and browser are stored only as salted one-way hashes for rate limiting.

Analytics

This site does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. No cookie banner is shown because none is needed: only Django's own session and CSRF cookies are set, both strictly necessary for the site to function.

Data retention and access

Contact messages are visible only to the site owner through the private management dashboard. There is no self-service way to delete a message you've sent — email {{ SITE_NAME }} directly through the contact form if you'd like one removed.
